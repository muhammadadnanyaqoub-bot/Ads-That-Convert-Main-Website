FAQ

Questions agencies ask us

Are you really 100% white-label? +

Yes. We never contact your clients or brand anything as ATC. Reports, dashboards, decks and comms all carry your identity. To your clients, we are your team.

Which niches do you specialize in? +

Local & service-based businesses — roofing, HVAC, home remodeling, plumbing, solar, med spa, dental, real estate, legal, and similar. We know what makes these leads convert into booked jobs.

How is pricing structured? +

A flat wholesale fulfillment fee per account. You decide what to charge clients at retail and keep the difference. Rates drop as your account volume grows.

How fast can we get started? +

Most partners are onboarded within a few business days. Once we have access and brand assets, we build strategy and get campaigns live shortly after.

Is there a long-term contract? +

No heavy lock-ins. We earn your business month to month by delivering booked jobs. Most partners stay because it works.
