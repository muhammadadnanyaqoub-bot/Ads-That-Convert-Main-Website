Why roofing leads behave differently

Roofing is not like most home services, and the ad account has to respect that. Four things change how we build and manage a roofing campaign:

  • ✓Seasonality. Demand moves with weather and time of year. A campaign that prints leads in spring can stall in deep winter, so budgets and offers have to flex month to month.
  • ✓Storm demand. After a hail or wind event, intent spikes in one area for a short window. Being in market within hours — not days — is what wins those jobs.
  • ✓High ticket value. A roof is a five-figure decision. Homeowners compare quotes and need trust before they book, so volume alone is not the goal — qualified roofing lead generation is.
  • ✓Speed-to-lead. Roofing leads go cold fast. If the client cannot reach the homeowner within minutes, booked roofing estimates drop. We route leads so the client can act immediately.

What our white label Meta ads for roofing include

Everything runs under your brand. A roofing account with us covers:

  • ✓Meta lead forms. Instant forms tuned to filter tyre-kickers and capture what the client needs to quote — so you get booked roofing estimates, not junk contacts.
  • ✓Storm-response campaigns. Pre-built structures we switch on for a specific area right after a weather event, while intent is high.
  • ✓Offer angles. Free inspection, financing, insurance-claim help, seasonal maintenance — we test the angle that fits each client’s market instead of guessing.
  • ✓Appointment funnels. The path from Facebook ads for roofers to a scheduled estimate on the client’s calendar, with fast lead delivery and follow-up prompts.
  • ✓Creative direction. Briefs for statics and video that show real roofs, real crews, and a clear offer.
  • ✓White-label reporting. Clean reports your account manager sends with your logo.

Roofing FAQ for agencies

Do you contact our roofing clients directly? +

No. We stay fully white-label. Every report, message, and deliverable carries your brand — your client only ever deals with you.

How fast can you launch a storm-response campaign? +

Once we have ad account access and brand assets, we can usually switch a storm campaign on within a day, so the client is in market while demand is high.

How much ad spend does a roofing account need? +

It depends on the market, the offer, and how many jobs the client can handle. We recommend a sensible starting budget on the call and scale from what the data shows.

Your client or your team closes. We deliver leads fast and can set up follow-up automation, but the calling and booking stays on your side — that is where speed-to-lead wins jobs.
